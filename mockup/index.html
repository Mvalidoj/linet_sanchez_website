Between memory, absence and imagination.

Through photography, sculpture and video, Linet Sánchez explores the architecture of memory and the spaces between remembered experience and imagined reality.

Artistic practice

My practice explores the relationship between memory, architecture and the spaces we inhabit, both physically and mentally.

Through photography, sculpture and video, I construct and examine empty environments that exist somewhere between remembered experience and imagined reality.
